JAPANESE RIVERSIDE COLORING BOOKMARK
1 design / black-and-white line art / digital files

1. Choose Japanese_Riverside_A4.pdf for A4 paper, or Japanese_Riverside_Letter.pdf for US Letter paper.
2. Print at 100% or Actual Size. Turn OFF Fit to Page / Shrink to Fit.
3. Measure the calibration line. It should be 50 mm (1.97 inches).
4. Cut along the three rectangular borders. Each bookmark is 50 x 150 mm.
5. Color the bookmarks. The three copies on a page are the same design.
Use paper or cardstock compatible with your printer. Scissors, paper and pencils are not included. Handle cutting tools carefully.
